Summary

Anyone may browse and search TogoVar. Before using information obtained from TogoVar, determine which of the following categories it belongs to.

  • TogoVar-original materials: Explanatory text, metadata, and the selection, arrangement, and structure of the database created by TogoVar and for which TogoVar holds the rights are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).
  • Third-party data: Data incorporated into TogoVar from other providers remains subject to the terms specified by each provider.

CC BY 4.0 applies only to the extent that TogoVar holds the relevant rights. If the terms for third-party data differ, follow the terms specified by the provider of that data.

License for TogoVar-original materials

Explanatory text, metadata, and the selection, arrangement, and structure of the database created by TogoVar and for which TogoVar holds the rights may be used under CC BY 4.0.

Why the license changed from ODbL 1.0 to CC BY 4.0

TogoVar-original materials were previously provided under ODbL 1.0. Beginning with release 2026.1, they are provided under CC BY 4.0.

CC BY 4.0 permits sharing and adaptation provided that appropriate credit is given, a link to the license is provided, and changes are indicated. By contrast, the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 includes a Share-Alike requirement: if an adapted database is publicly used, that adapted database must also be offered under the ODbL. TogoVar uses CC BY 4.0 rather than the ODbL for TogoVar-original materials to make them easier to incorporate into research outputs, documentation, and other databases, while clearly distinguishing them from third-party data that remains subject to each provider's terms.

Under CC BY 4.0, you may do the following for commercial and non-commercial purposes:

  • Share: Copy and redistribute the materials.
  • Adapt: Modify the materials and incorporate them into other works or databases.

When using these materials, you must comply with the following conditions:

  • Attribution: Cite the TogoVar article below and provide a link to CC BY 4.0.
  • Indication of changes: Indicate whether you modified the materials.
